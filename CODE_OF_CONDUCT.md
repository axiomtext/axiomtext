# Code of Conduct

AxiomText follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and welcoming, assume good intent, give constructive feedback, and no harassment of any kind.

Report unacceptable behaviour to **hello@axiomtext.dev**. All reports are handled confidentially, and maintainers will
take fair, appropriate action.
